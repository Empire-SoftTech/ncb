Who the Veteran Transition template is for

Veteran Transition suits mid-level and senior candidates in corporate, finance, consulting and management roles. Purpose-built for military veterans entering civilian careers. Translates service experience into civilian language.

Why this layout works

A restrained typographic hierarchy that puts achievements before duties, so a hiring manager skimming for 20 seconds lands on results first.

ATS performance

Veteran Transition scores 91/100 in NextCV's ATS analyser, meaning its structure parses cleanly through the automated screening most large employers run before human review.

Its 91/100 puts it second of the 5 professional templates, just behind Legal & Compliance at 92. That places it in the top 38% of the 29-template library for machine readability.

Pricing

Veteran Transition is a premium template, unlocked with a single one-time purchase of ₹19 (down from ₹149) — not a subscription. Once unlocked it stays available across every device you sign in on, and exports carry no watermark.

Tip for the Professional format

Lead every bullet with a verb and close it with a number. "Cut onboarding time 40%" outperforms "Responsible for onboarding".

Use the live ATS score while editing rather than at the end; fixing structure early is far quicker than reworking a finished draft.
