Your Privacy

Delete Your Account

You have the right to permanently delete your NextCV account and all associated data at any time — no questions asked.

Takes < 2 minutes Data erased within 30 days Irreversible action
Permanent action — cannot be undone

Once you delete your account, all your resumes, templates, and personal data will be permanently removed. You will lose access immediately and data will be erased within 30 days. This action cannot be reversed.

Step-by-Step Deletion Guide

Follow these steps inside the NextCV app on your Android device.

  1. 1

    Open the NextCV App

    Launch the NextCV app on your Android device. Make sure you are signed in to the account you wish to delete.

Can't Access the App?

If you no longer have access to the app or are unable to follow the steps above, you can request account deletion via email:

1
Send an email to support@nextcvbuilder.online
2
Use subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
3
Include your registered email address and Google account name
4
We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 7 business days

What Gets Deleted

  • All saved resumes
  • Exported PDF files
  • Profile information
  • ATS analysis history
  • App settings & preferences
  • Authentication & session tokens

What Is NOT Affected

  • PDFs already saved to your phone
  • PDFs emailed / shared externally
  • Your Google account

Think Twice First

Before deleting, consider these alternatives:

Export your resumes Download all resumes as PDFs before deleting
Disable notifications Turn off all alerts in Settings → Notifications
Just sign out Keep your data safe — just sign out of the app
