This Privacy Policy applies to the NextCV – AI Resume Builder mobile application and website (nextcvbuilder.online), operated by Empire Softtech ("we", "our", or "us"). By using NextCV, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

01 Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account and build your resume, we collect:

  • Account information: Full name, email address, profile photo (from Google Sign-In)
  • Resume content: Personal details, work experience, education history, skills, certifications, and any other information you enter into your resume
  • Contact information: Phone number, address, LinkedIn URL, portfolio URL (as entered in your resume)
  • Communications: Messages you send to our support team

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the app, we automatically collect:

  • Device information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage data: Features used, screens visited, time spent in app, crash reports
  • Log data: IP address, app version, timestamps of actions
  • Analytics data: Via Firebase Analytics — aggregated, anonymized usage patterns

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account
  • Firebase services: Authentication tokens, crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics
We do not collect payment information. NextCV is free to use and we do not process any financial transactions.

02 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

Resume Generation

To generate, format, and export your professional resumes as PDF documents

AI Suggestions

To provide AI-powered content suggestions tailored to your job title and experience

ATS Scoring

To analyze your resume and provide an ATS compatibility score with improvement suggestions

App Improvement

To understand how features are used, fix bugs, and improve the overall experience

Customer Support

To respond to your questions, troubleshoot issues, and provide assistance

Security

To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security vulnerabilities

We do not use your resume content to train AI models. We do not send you marketing emails without your consent.

03 Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

3.1 Service Providers

We use trusted third-party services to operate NextCV. These providers access your data only to perform tasks on our behalf:

Provider Purpose Data Shared
Google Firebase Authentication, analytics, crash reporting Email, device info, crash logs
Google Cloud Cloud infrastructure, data storage Resume data (encrypted)
OpenAI / AI Provider AI-generated content suggestions Job title, section context only (no PII)

3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government authority, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our legal rights or prevent harm.

3.3 Business Transfers

If Empire Softtech is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

04 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between the app and our servers uses TLS 1.3
  • Encryption at rest: Database records are encrypted using AES-256
  • Authentication tokens: Stored securely using Flutter Secure Storage (Android Keystore)
  • Access control: Strict role-based access — our team cannot read your resume content without explicit permission
  • Regular security audits: We review our infrastructure and code for vulnerabilities periodically
No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. While we use commercially reasonable measures, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

05 Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you

Correction

Update or correct inaccurate personal information

Deletion

Delete your account and all associated data permanently from app Settings → Account → Delete Account

Data Portability

Export your resume data in a machine-readable format

Opt-Out

Opt out of analytics collection via app Settings → Privacy → Analytics

Object

Object to processing of your data for specific purposes

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@nextcvbuilder.online. We will respond within 30 days.

06 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specifically:

  • Active account data: Retained for the lifetime of your account
  • Deleted account data: Permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
  • Resume PDFs: Deleted immediately upon account deletion
  • Analytics data: Retained in anonymized, aggregated form for up to 24 months
  • Support communications: Retained for 12 months after resolution
  • Legal/compliance records: Retained as required by applicable law

We use soft deletes only — your data is flagged as deleted and queued for permanent removal. We never hard-delete user data before the 30-day grace period, allowing account recovery if deletion was accidental.

07 Children's Privacy

NextCV is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us immediately at support@nextcvbuilder.online and we will delete the information.

08 Cookies and Tracking

The NextCV mobile app does not use browser cookies. Our website (nextcvbuilder.online) may use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function correctly
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics to understand visitor behavior (anonymized)

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your ability to use any part of the website.

09 International Data Transfers

NextCV is operated by Empire Softtech based in India. By using the app, you agree that your data may be transferred to and processed in India and other countries where our service providers operate (including Google Cloud servers in the US and EU). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for all international transfers.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Send an in-app notification to all users
  • Require re-acknowledgement for material changes affecting your rights

Continued use of NextCV after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
