This Privacy Policy applies to the NextCV – AI Resume Builder mobile application and website (nextcvbuilder.online), operated by Empire Softtech ("we", "our", or "us"). By using NextCV, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

This Privacy Policy governs the NextCV application and website, a service provided, developed, and fully operated by Empire Softtech. All application assets, user data parameters, and trademarks belong exclusively to Empire Softtech.

01 Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account and build your resume, we collect:

  • Account information: Full name, email address, profile photo (from Google Sign-In)
  • Resume content: Personal details, work experience, education history, skills, certifications, and any other information you enter into your resume
  • Contact information: Phone number, address, LinkedIn URL, portfolio URL (as entered in your resume)
  • Communications: Messages you send to our support team

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the app, we automatically collect:

  • Device information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage data: Features used, screens visited, time spent in app, crash reports
  • Log data: IP address, app version, timestamps of actions
  • Analytics data: Via Firebase Analytics — aggregated, anonymized usage patterns
  • Advertising and device identifiers: Where advertising is enabled, information such as your advertising ID (e.g., Google Advertising ID), IP address, and app/ad interaction data may be processed to support ad delivery, analytics, and fraud prevention — see Section 03: Advertising and Google AdMob

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account
  • Firebase services: Authentication tokens, crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Where advertising is enabled, Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK may provide device, advertising, and ad-interaction information to support ad delivery and measurement
  • Payment providers: Where paid features are enabled, Google Play or another authorized payment provider may share transaction-related information with us — see Section 04: Purchases and Premium Features
NextCV does not itself store your complete credit/debit card number. NextCV may offer optional paid digital features (see Section 04: Purchases and Premium Features), and any purchase is processed through Google Play or another authorized payment provider.

02 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

Resume Generation

To generate, format, and export your professional resumes as PDF documents

AI Suggestions

To provide AI-powered content suggestions tailored to your job title and experience

ATS Scoring

To analyze your resume and provide an ATS compatibility score with improvement suggestions

App Improvement

To understand how features are used, fix bugs, and improve the overall experience

Customer Support

To respond to your questions, troubleshoot issues, and provide assistance

Security

To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security vulnerabilities

We do not use your resume content to train AI models. We do not send you marketing emails without your consent. Where advertising is enabled, we may also use limited technical information (such as device and advertising identifiers) to display and measure ads through Google AdMob — see Section 03 below.

03 Advertising and Google AdMob

NextCV may use Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising within the app. When advertising is enabled, depending on the SDK configuration, your consent choices, your device settings, and applicable law, advertising services may process information such as:

  • IP address
  • Advertising and device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version)
  • App interactions (screens viewed, features used)
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks)
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and ad delivery

Depending on your consent, applicable law, device settings, and configuration, advertising shown in NextCV may be personalized or non-personalized. We do not claim that personalized advertising is always used.

3.1 Rewarded Ads

NextCV may use rewarded ads to provide an in-app benefit, such as access to an eligible resume download or feature, in exchange for voluntarily watching an ad. Rewarded ads do not provide cash or any direct monetary compensation. Rewarded ads may not be enabled in all versions of the app.

NextCV does not sell your resume content to advertisers. Advertising and device identifiers processed for ad delivery are separate from the personal resume content you create in NextCV.

For more information on how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, see Google's Partner Sites policy.

04 Purchases and Premium Features

NextCV may offer optional paid digital features or premium resume templates. Where purchase functionality is enabled, purchases are processed through Google Play or another authorized payment provider — NextCV does not directly collect or store your complete credit/debit card number.

When you make a purchase, we may receive transaction-related information from the payment provider, such as:

  • Product or template purchased
  • Transaction ID
  • Purchase date
  • Currency
  • Purchase amount
  • Purchase or subscription status

This information is used to verify purchases, provide access to purchased features, and for customer support and fraud prevention. Purchase functionality may not be live in all versions of the app; where it is not yet available, this section describes how it will operate once enabled.

05 Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

5.1 Service Providers

We use trusted third-party services to operate NextCV. These providers access your data only to perform tasks on our behalf. Some rows below — such as advertising and payment providers — apply only where the corresponding feature is enabled in your version of the app:

Provider Purpose Data Shared
Google Firebase Authentication, analytics, crash reporting Account, device, analytics, and diagnostic information
Google Mobile Ads / AdMob Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention (where advertising is enabled) IP address, device/advertising identifiers, ad and app interactions, diagnostic information

5.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government authority, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our legal rights or prevent harm.

5.3 Business Transfers

If Empire Softtech is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

06 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between the app and our servers uses TLS 1.3
  • Encryption at rest: Database records are encrypted using AES-256
  • Authentication tokens: Stored securely using Flutter Secure Storage (Android Keystore)
  • Access control: Strict role-based access — our team cannot read your resume content without explicit permission
  • Regular security audits: We review our infrastructure and code for vulnerabilities periodically
No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. While we use commercially reasonable measures, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07 Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you

Correction

Update or correct inaccurate personal information

Deletion

Delete your account and all associated data permanently from app Settings → Account → Delete Account

Data Portability

Export your resume data in a machine-readable format

Opt-Out

Opt out of analytics collection via app Settings → Privacy → Analytics

Object

Object to processing of your data for specific purposes

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@nextcvbuilder.online. We will respond within 30 days.

08 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specifically:

  • Active account data: Retained for the lifetime of your account
  • Deleted account data: Permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
  • Resume PDFs: Deleted immediately upon account deletion
  • Analytics data: Retained in anonymized, aggregated form for up to 24 months
  • Support communications: Retained for 12 months after resolution
  • Legal/compliance records: Retained as required by applicable law

We use soft deletes only — your data is flagged as deleted and queued for permanent removal. We never hard-delete user data before the 30-day grace period, allowing account recovery if deletion was accidental.

09 Children's Privacy

NextCV is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us immediately at support@nextcvbuilder.online and we will delete the information.

10 Cookies and Tracking

The NextCV mobile app does not use browser cookies. Where Google AdMob is enabled, the SDK may use advertising identifiers and similar technologies (rather than browser cookies) within the app to support ad delivery and measurement — see Section 03: Advertising and Google AdMob. Our website (nextcvbuilder.online) may use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function correctly
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics to understand visitor behavior (anonymized)

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your ability to use any part of the website.

11 International Data Transfers

NextCV is operated by Empire Softtech based in India. By using the app, you agree that your data may be transferred to and processed in India and other countries where our service providers operate (including Google Cloud servers in the US and EU). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for all international transfers.

12 Privacy Choices and Consent

Where legally required, NextCV will provide appropriate consent and privacy-choice mechanisms — such as a consent prompt — before processing data for advertising or other applicable purposes described in this policy.

Depending on your consent, applicable law, your device settings, and app configuration, advertising shown in NextCV may be personalized or non-personalized. You may also be able to manage ad personalization through your device's ad settings (for example, Google Settings → Ads on Android) and through in-app privacy settings where available.

For more details on how Google uses information from apps that use Google services, see Google's Partner Sites policy.

13 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Send an in-app notification to all users
  • Require re-acknowledgement for material changes affecting your rights

Continued use of NextCV after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
